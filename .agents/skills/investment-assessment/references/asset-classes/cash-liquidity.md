# Asset class: Cash & liquidity

## Job

Emergency fund, dry powder, liability matching, ballast when risk assets are rich or uncertain.

## Diligence checklist

- [ ] Instrument: demand deposit, term deposit, T-bill, money-market fund, short gov fund  
- [ ] Principal protection claim (deposit insurance limits if any)  
- [ ] Net yield after tax  
- [ ] Breakage / early withdrawal penalties  
- [ ] Ladder vs bullet for known cash dates  
- [ ] Counterparty (bank/fund) quality  
- [ ] FX if wealth measured in another currency  

## Sizing

- Floor = living expenses × months needed + near-term known outflows  
- Do **not** fill “cash job” with HY credit, equity, or crypto  

## Regime notes

- Tight liquidity / risk-off: cash optionality valuable  
- High inflation: real yield may be negative — still hold **floor**; excess can rotate to real assets per rails  
- Do not abandon cash floor to chase gold or yield under panic without a written plan  

## Comparables

Term deposits vs short bond funds vs T-bills: trade yield vs mark-to-market vs convenience.
