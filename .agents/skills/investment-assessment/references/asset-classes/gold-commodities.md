# Asset class: Gold & commodities

## Job

**Insurance / real purchasing-power hedge**, diversifier vs financial assets, sometimes cycle (industrial metals). Gold is not a coupon engine.

## Diligence checklist (gold)

- [ ] Form: bar, coin, jewelry, allocated account, ETF, certificate — custody & counterparty  
- [ ] Premium/spread vs global spot (local market premia matter)  
- [ ] Storage, insurance, assay, buyback liquidity  
- [ ] Tax on purchase/sale (jurisdiction)  
- [ ] Policy risk (import, monopoly, forced sale rules)  

## Diligence checklist (other commodities)

- [ ] Roll yield / futures curve if via futures/ETF  
- [ ] Storage and seasonality  
- [ ] Demand drivers (industrial vs food vs energy)  

## Sizing

- Size to **hedge need and fear budget**, not to match a bond’s yield  
- Under explicit debasement/collapse narrative: fund gold **before** HY carry satellites  
- Rule of thumb only: many investors use a **band** (e.g. mid-single to low-double-digit % NW) — override with rails  

## Regime notes

- Inflation scare, geopolitical stress, easy money after shock: often supportive  
- Strong real rates + risk-on liquidity: can lag  
- Central-bank buying: multi-year structural bid possible; **not** a guaranteed 2y IRR  
- **Coupling:** paths that boost gold demand often **stress** HY credit and high-beta risk — poor substitute pair  

## Anti-patterns

- “Gold must beat 10% bond or it’s bad” — wrong job  
- All-in gold with multi-decade growth goals and no compounding engine  
- Ignoring local premium and sell spreads  
- Unallocated paper gold without understanding counterparty
