# Asset class: Funds & ETFs

## Job

Diversified exposure (equity, bond, balanced, thematic) with professional packaging. Liquidity usually better than single private names.

## Diligence checklist

- [ ] Mandate & benchmark  
- [ ] Holdings concentration / active share  
- [ ] Total expense ratio + loads + exit fees  
- [ ] Tracking error / realization of strategy  
- [ ] AUM, liquidity of underlying, gates/swing pricing  
- [ ] Manager tenure, style drift history  
- [ ] Tax treatment of distributions (jurisdiction-specific)  
- [ ] Replication method (physical/synthetic) for ETFs  
- [ ] Currency hedging policy  

## Sizing

- Core fund sleeves can be large if diversified and liquid  
- Thematic/sector funds: treat closer to satellite; watch overlap with stocks you already own  
- Bond funds ≠ single-name credit: lower idiosyncratic default, still mark-to-market rate risk  

## Comparison rules

- Compare **net-of-fee** paths to single names and to deposits  
- Do not assume past 1Y return persists  
- Liquidity of fund ≠ liquidity of underlying in a gate event (read rules)

## Regime notes

- Duration-heavy bond funds hurt when rates spike  
- Equity funds follow beta regime  
- “Stable growth” bond funds are ballast candidates; HY funds are risk-on credit beta
