# Asset class: Crypto

## Job

Speculative / high-volatility asymmetric growth, sometimes “hard money” narrative sleeve. **Not** cash, **not** ballast.

## Diligence checklist

- [ ] Asset: BTC, ETH, major L1/L2, alt — liquidity tier  
- [ ] Custody: self-custody vs exchange (counterparty + opsec)  
- [ ] Venue regulation, withdrawal history, proof-of-reserves claims (skeptically)  
- [ ] Smart-contract / bridge / stablecoin depeg risk if DeFi  
- [ ] Token unlocks, inflation schedule, governance attack surface  
- [ ] On-chain yield source (real fees vs emissions ponzi)  
- [ ] Tax lot tracking complexity  
- [ ] Correlation with Nasdaq/risk-on in stress (often high)  

## Sizing

- Entire crypto sleeve ≤ max pain on severe drawdown (−50% to −90% historically possible)  
- Alts << majors inside the sleeve unless intentional  
- Never fund life liquidity from crypto  
- Position adds: predefine invalidation; avoid leverage unless expert and pain-sized  

## Regime notes

- Risk-on / excess liquidity: tailwinds  
- Tightening / credit stress: often simultaneous equity-like crash  
- Narrative “hedge against fiat” can fail short/medium term vs gold  
- Do not assume crypto diversifies a high-beta equity book in crash  

## Anti-patterns

- Yield farming APY without understanding emissions  
- Exchange-only custody of size you can’t lose  
- Averaging down unlocked VC bags  
- Treating stablecoins as risk-free cash without issuer/depeg analysis
