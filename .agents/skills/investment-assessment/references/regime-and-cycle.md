# Regime and market cycle

Purpose: make the **macro/micro backdrop explicit** so asset choices are conditional, not absolute.

## Dimensions (score qualitatively)

| Dimension | Labels (examples) | Assets often sensitive |
|---|---|---|
| Real growth | Boom / trend / stall / contraction | Cyclical equity, credit spreads, commodities industrial |
| Inflation | Falling / stable / rising / regime-shift | Duration, gold, real assets, some commodities |
| Policy rates / liquidity | Easy / neutral / tight / crisis easing | Multiples, HY credit, housing-sensitive |
| FX (local vs hard) | Stable crawl / orderly dep / gap risk | Local bonds, local equity vs USD wealth |
| Risk appetite | Risk-on / mixed / risk-off | Beta, crypto, HY, EM |
| Credit cycle | Expansion / late / stress / repair | HY, private credit, banks, consumer lenders |
| Micro (issuer/sector) | Moat improving / stable / disrupted | Single names, sector funds |

You need a **base** combination + at least one **stress flip** (e.g. base soft landing → stress stagflation or credit event).

## Transmission cheat-sheet (directional, not destiny)

| Shift | Often supports | Often pressures |
|---|---|---|
| Disinflation + soft growth | Duration, quality equity | Cyclicals, high leverage credit |
| Re-acceleration growth | Cyclical equity, industrial commodities | Long duration if rates reprice up |
| Inflation scare | Gold, real assets, short duration | Long nominal bonds, some growth equity |
| Tight liquidity / risk-off | Cash, quality, sometimes gold | HY credit, small caps, crypto |
| Local FX gap risk | Hard assets, FX-earning equity | Local unsecured fixed coupon (in hard-currency lens) |
| Credit stress | Senior secured / gov, cash | Unsecured HY, consumer lenders, refinance-heavy issuers |

## Micro vs macro

- **Macro** sets the weather (which factor sleeves make sense).  
- **Micro** picks the vehicle (which issuer/fund/token survives the weather).  
- Good micro in bad credit weather still dies; bad micro in good weather still disappoints.

## Coupling traps

1. **Gold fear + HY carry** — same stress often lifts gold and hurts HY.  
2. **Crypto + high-beta equity** — often same risk-on factor.  
3. **Local deposits + local bonds** — both fail the hard-currency lens together.  
4. **Employer equity + sector funds** — income and portfolio same shock.

## Process

1. State base regime in one paragraph.  
2. List 2–4 sleeves favored / unfavored.  
3. State stress regime and what breaks.  
4. Only then size the opportunity.  
5. Revisit when a **trigger** hits (policy surprise, CPI print regime-change, credit event, FX gap).

## What not to do

- Treat one news headline as a full regime.  
- Require false precision (exact CPI to 1 decimal) when a band suffices.  
- Use regime to justify unlimited concentration.  
- Ignore micro terms because “macro is great.”
