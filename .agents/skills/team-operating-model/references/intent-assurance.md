# Intent assurance without exhaustive owner review

Use for substantial delivery with an existing outcome, scope, independent checking
and action authority. This is part of the current delivery loop, not a permanent
committee or a new approval layer. Small settled tasks need proportional evidence;
this reference does not activate a team on its own.

## Before substantial generation

Retain original requests/clarifications and cumulative constraints separately from
mutable plans. In the existing task record, name outcome, must-not-change boundaries,
governing source versions, material assumptions and in/out scope. Include a desired
example and the nearest plausible wrong example. Resolve consequential ambiguity
before dependent work, not every local implementation detail.

Distinguish normative requirements from current code, descriptive docs, drafts and
superseded guidance. When they materially conflict, return locations, status,
impact/options and a recommendation immediately on discovery; stop only affected
work. Explicit approved supersession is not a new question. Reconcile affected
links/indexes/owned outputs with the change, preserving unrelated/user work.

## Allocate assurance, not more ceremony

- **Delivery:** Build and run genuine checks within settled bounds. Report
  deviations and missing proof; do not redefine success to match an implementation.
- **Independent intent conformance:** The existing dedicated checker reconciles
  every cumulative original requirement with the current plan/result, including
  audience/style/format and constraints—not just the builder's summary.
- **Behavior verification:** Assign responsibility for critical positive, failure
  and distinguishing-case proof. Use a separate verifier where required by the
  contract or consequences; the builder's assertion alone is not independent proof.
  One bounded existing QA assignment can cover compatible checking duties, without
  validating its own authored artifact or replacing mandatory independence.

Requirements, acceptance, scope and risk changes use the existing decision route.
The delivery lead cannot suppress dissent. Specialist review is consequence-driven;
a risk label never waives mandatory reviews, human slots or other controls.
Absent an explicitly authorized different policy, retain the existing approval
floor. Do not copy a vendor's risk tiers or automatic-merge rights into permission.

## Return one exception-first card

Use this compact shape in the existing handoff; omit empty routine detail, not
required gaps. Keep raw evidence discoverable below it, without owner reconstruction.

```text
Intent → observed result: governing request/decision + exact artifact revision.
Exceptions: changed assumptions; removed/substituted/deferred behavior and authority;
            unresolved conflicts, disagreement, failures and missing evidence.
Critical proof: 1–2 distinguishing probes, expected vs actual + raw evidence links.
               For each critical claim: executed/failed/skipped/unavailable; scope.
Decision: exact unresolved choice + consequences/options/recommendation, or none.
```

A probe is a real behavior observation when executed, not a rewritten claim. A
successful command with critical skips does not prove those paths. A completed
component check is not proof of a promised end-to-end outcome. Missing proof
is UNVERIFIED; technical success cannot hide a failed intent requirement. Owners
may select additional probes or inspect raw detail; summaries are not a trust boundary.

## Correct, recover and learn

Repair in-scope defects without routine owner micromanagement. A change to outcome,
acceptance, scope or material risk needs an explicit what/why/impact/options/
recommendation proposal through its authority. QA pass grants neither acceptance
nor merge, deploy, spend or publication rights.

Tie approvals and proof to actual source/artifact/configuration versions. Recheck
affected claims after changes; do not reuse stale green evidence. For authorized
release work, identify detection signals, rollback/disable path and accountable
recovery owner before relying on rapid repair. Recovery plans do not authorize live
effects or make irreversible actions safe.

Diagnose repeated or alternating repair findings rather than repeating patches.
Step back by the third review/repair round at latest, earlier for a repeated
fingerprint or broken verifier. Honor any stricter contracted review budget—this
does not add retries, reset budgets, impose a total task cap or force a human stop
when a safe authorized revised approach exists. Recheck changed/failed surfaces;
do not repeatedly flag unchanged accepted or unrelated pre-existing code.

When changing/relying on an automated reviewer, require a calibrated review
capability: representative known-defective and known-clean inputs, independent
judgments, false acceptance and false-positive harm checks, and measured effort.
If unavailable, disclose the gap and do not relax established scrutiny. Product
failures and false reviewer findings should inform authorized future evaluations;
do not automatically capture private incidents, expand access or schedule jobs.

Pilot a capability, not report volume. Observe owner review + repair time, intent
misunderstandings caught before delivery, later rework/escaped failures and reviewer
noise. Preserve null results and confounders; stop any unsafe relaxation. Implemented
guidance and synthetic exercises do not demonstrate production benefit.

**Prototype:** 2026-10-23; assess after 1–2 real deliveries. No new service,
continuous surveillance, risk-tier policy or automatic approval is supplied here.
