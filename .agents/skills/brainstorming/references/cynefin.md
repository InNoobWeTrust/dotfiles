# Cynefin — optional ideation context lens

Use when uncertainty or mixed contexts make the next learning step unclear, not
as a compulsory classification before ideation. Inputs are the unresolved outcome,
observed conditions, evidence, constraints and settled decisions. Output is a
provisional learning posture and bounded experiment proposal, with disconfirmers
and a review point. This lens does not authorize action or replace an owning method.

## Framework, not method

Cynefin is a sense-making and decision-support framework, not an ideation method,
predictive model or difficulty score. **Bounded applicability** means useful
approaches have contextual limits. Its five domains are not Cartesian quadrants
or a fixed taxonomy: conditions change, and parts of one question can occupy
different domains. Classify the situation, never people, teams or their competence.

Cynefin classifies the situation; it grants no action authority. In a chaotic context, only if the human owner has explicitly authorized the specific containment scope, stabilize minimally within that scope and then reassess; otherwise escalate without acting. Urgency alone is not evidence of chaos. Containment and probes never establish verified resolution.

## Retrieve cues, then challenge the fit

The local [domain catalog](../assets/cynefin-domains.csv) lives at
`assets/cynefin-domains.csv`, resolved from the loaded skill root. Inspect its
header; use an available authorized text or CSV search to retrieve a few rows by
observed cues or keywords. Check `disconfirmers` and `posture` before using a row;
a search hit does not establish applicability. Without search, use header and
known-ID lookup: `clear`, `complicated`, `complex`, `chaotic`, `confusion`. No
installation, new store or catalog upload is needed or authorized.

Bound the question and time window, separate observations from hypotheses, and
state what would change your provisional posture. Unknown does not mean complex:
a missing source may call for research or expert analysis, not an intervention.
Urgent does not mean chaotic, and disagreement alone establishes neither. If the
context remains mixed or confused, decompose the question and gather evidence
rather than forcing one label. Reassess after evidence or conditions change.

## Keep the chosen outcome path

- **Creative exploration:** retain framing, divergence, clustering, convergence
  and evaluation. Use the lens only to distinguish reuse of an established
  practice, an analytical question, or a bounded learning probe; do not turn it
  into a mandatory classification workshop or idea quota.
- **Scientific discovery:** retain entity/modality checks, at least three competing
  hypothesis angles, causal links, four-axis rigor, negative controls and kill
  criteria. A complex-context probe still needs falsifiable hypotheses and
  appropriate controls; emergence is not an excuse to abandon scientific rigor.
- **Settled product intent:** preserve the chosen direction and requested brief.
  Apply the lens only to unresolved assumptions and learning proposals; do not
  restart divergence or choose a new product direction.

Clear concerns favor established practice with checked applicability; complicated
concerns favor research and expert analysis. Complex concerns favor proposals for
bounded hypothesis probes, not prediction by linear analysis alone. Chaotic
concerns follow the authority contract above, not an ideation-driven intervention.
Confusion calls for decomposition and evidence. A technical diagnosis or durable
repair needs the appropriate investigation capability with causal evidence and
verification; if unavailable, return the gap, not a verified root-cause claim.

### Bounded probe proposal

Keep the proposal compact in the selected path's existing output:

- **Hypothesis:** mechanism or pattern, competing explanation and disconfirming
  observation; retain scientific negative controls and kill criteria when relevant.
- **Owner and authority:** accountable owner and required execution permission.
  Brainstorming, a brief or a domain label grants none.
- **Limits:** affected surface, permitted intervention, time/resources and bounded
  downside. If harm cannot be bounded, propose a safer check or escalate.
- **Observation:** baseline where available, signals and interpretation limits.
- **Stop/rollback:** thresholds, responsible owner and feasible recovery path.
- **Review:** when/who reviews results and reassesses the posture; retain negative
  results. A promising signal does not prove a causal explanation or grant scale-up.

Missing evidence or capability remains an explicit proposal gap; do not fabricate
sources, run compute/laboratory work, alter live systems or install tooling.
Containment is not proof of a hypothesis, and neither containment nor a probe is
verified resolution. Propose next learning steps without claiming their execution.
