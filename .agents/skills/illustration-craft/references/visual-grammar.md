# Visual Grammar

## Hierarchy

Order of attention should generally be:

1. headline / dominant takeaway
2. focal illustration or main comparison
3. supporting annotations
4. footnotes, sources, and caveats

## Typography

- Use clear size contrast between headline, subhead, body, and caption.
- Keep font families limited.
- Do not shrink text to fit a bad layout; fix the layout.

## Color

- Use restrained palettes.
- Reserve saturated accent colors for emphasis.
- Keep neutrals for structure and secondary context.
- Ensure contrast remains readable.

## Icons and shapes

- Icons must carry meaning, not act as filler.
- Use one visual style consistently.
- Pair icons with labels when interpretation could vary.

## Spacing and alignment

- Align to an invisible grid.
- Keep gutters and padding consistent.
- Do not let elements nearly touch; either align them or separate them clearly.
