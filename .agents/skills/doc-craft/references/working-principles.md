# Editorial Working Principles

Use for clear communication across technical and non-technical writing. These principles do not impose the full technical-document workflow on marketing copy, comments, or governance files; retain the owning skill and audience-specific contract.

For delegated work, the primary establishes discovery and the action/evidence contract through [Subagent Dispatch](../../subagent-dispatch/SKILL.md). Workers select and load useful principles themselves; parent references or short hints are optional. Do not assume inherited context or permissions.

## Editing

### Thinking Principles

- **Clarity Over Volume**: Communicate with precision. Eliminate fluff, redundancy, and passive jargon. The most effective documentation explains concepts in the fewest words necessary for complete clarity.
- **Progressive Disclosure**: Layer information logically from high-level overview to operational usage and deep reference.
- **Proportionality**: Calibrate documentation depth strictly to the subject. Small utilities require concise, focused guides rather than multi-document sharding.
- **Single Source of Truth**: Link to authoritative definitions and specifications rather than duplicating content across files where it can drift.
- **Reader, Not Conversation Partner**: Establish who will use the artifact, what they need to do, and what knowledge or references they can reasonably have. Include necessary explanation; omit unrelated conversation history and preserve the scope of account-, deployment-, and investigation-specific claims. Read the draft as someone without the chat: neither unexplained shared assumptions nor a transcript dump is acceptable.

### Operational Behavior

- **Paragraph-Level Reader Check**: For each paragraph, identify the reader action, decision, or necessary understanding it enables. Remove content that serves none; do not merely relocate it. Keep consumer instructions about use and constraints, and put implementation mechanics or design rationale in maintainer material only when maintenance requires it. Check usability without the conversation separately from unnecessary-context removal; preserve needed definitions, rationale, and safety facts.
- **Scannable Structure**: Use descriptive headings, tables, concise lists, and structured diagrams for complex flows to maximize scannability.
- **Grounded Examples**: Provide accurate, working examples that conform to actual project conventions, configurations, and command interfaces.
- **Focused Handoff**: Read enough authoritative context to preserve meaning, not every related document. Return the changed locations, material editorial decisions, validation evidence, and unresolved source questions; do not duplicate the finished document or dump the research trail into the parent's context.
