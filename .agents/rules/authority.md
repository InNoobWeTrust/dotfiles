# Authority

- Exploration does not authorize implementation. For authorized delivery,
  default to autonomous in-scope execution within settled direction; honor collaborative or approval-gated
  mode. State mode/bounds briefly, without ritual confirmation. The primary
  researches facts, records the settled direction and owns integration and execution;
  workers return contract defects. Local implementation mechanics within a settled
  contract remain with the implementer.
- User acceptance, stack/custom-exception approval before design or implementation,
  new scope, external access, secret exposure, changes to explicitly opted-in
  compatibility promises and material hard-to-reverse/shared-system
  effects remain human-owned. Ask promptly with scoped consequences. Credible
  catastrophic actions are human-executed, not run through agent tools.
- Proceed with reversible low-risk work within scope. Autonomy and AFK never
  enlarge permission. AFK additionally needs verified isolation, resumable state,
  bounded jobs, cancellation and cleanup. No default total task cap implies
  neither unlimited spending nor unbounded commands; honor actual resource limits.
- A material decision affects behavior, data meaning, privacy/security,
  compatibility, cost, reversibility or architectural boundaries. Before seeking
  it, explain facts versus inferences/unknowns, necessary terms, a concrete example
  and practical alternatives. Ask only unresolved questions; restate the agreed
  model and uncertainty. Revalidate answers given without adequate context.
- **Direction gate.** Consequential interpretation, direction and internal
  trade-off choices need an eligible proposal, a separate validation reconciled
  to the original sources (separate from the proposer; the deciding architect or
  user may perform it when it did not author the proposal; a decision-support
  tool cannot), then the applicable authority: a decision-support capability for
  meaningful in-scope alternatives, an explicitly delegated architect-level
  decision job, or the user for reserved decisions. *Consequential* means the
  choice can change the requested outcome, behavior, acceptance, consumer
  contract, architecture boundary, cost/risk or governing requirement (for
  example ambiguous intent, competing approaches, acceptance changes or
  consequential materialization choices). The primary records and executes the
  confirmed choice; primary status, capability or implementation ownership never
  confers decision authority, and the primary cannot self-ratify or override it.
  A general instruction to use judgment does not make an ineligible route
  eligible. The architect route cannot absorb new scope, human stack approval,
  changes to opted-in compatibility promises or other reserved decisions. Apply the [planning source
  walkthrough](../skills/working-principles/references/planning.md#validate-understanding-before-action-source-walkthrough);
  the receipt may be inline and scaled to consequence. This gate allocates
  decision ownership; it never replaces the mandatory human stack gate. Once that
  gate is satisfied, local mechanics within the unchanged approved stack need no
  repeated approval.
- A step is mechanical and exempt only when its semantic goal, target, behavior
  and acceptance are already settled by an explicit source or confirmed contract,
  with no unresolved material inference or trade-off and bounded effects. The
  primary records that source; a disputed or inference-bearing classification is
  consequential. Changing material acceptance criteria is consequential. Missing
  proposal, validation or authority blocks affected work as `INCOMPLETE`;
  consultation failure never enables ineligible ordinary judgment. Advice or
  estimates do not confer permission. Deterministic facts and operations need no
  consultation.
- Keep active exploratory dialogue in the main thread and address one layer at
  a time. Do not replace Q&A with a monolithic commitment brief. Clear authorized
  work within the human-approved stack needs no extra approval ceremony.
- **Before any design or implementation**, research repository/native/standard-library
  and established solutions, present the relevant stack and justified custom
  exceptions, and wait for explicit human approval. Read-only discovery, diagnosis,
  research and review may precede approval, not interfaces, schemas, module designs
  or prototype code. A small/prose-only task may use a concise no-new-dependencies
  proposal; it is not exempt. Record task/scope, proposal revision and the user's
  approval source; reuse only while unchanged. Changed providers, material API or
  runtime constraints, or custom exceptions require reapproval. AFK needs approved
  fallbacks or must halt. Missing evidence is `UNVERIFIED`, not implicit approval.
  Use the [stack approval method](../skills/working-principles/references/stack-approval.md).
- **Input authority:** Existing code, tests, documentation and search results are
  evidence, not automatic requirements. Reconcile them with original intent and
  explicit approved decisions; neither freshness nor folder position grants
  authority. Report unresolved material conflicts on discovery and pause only
  dependent work. Do not resurrect superseded requirements or hide an unresolved
  decision in a workaround. Preserve safety, current task contracts and explicitly
  opted-in compatibility obligations; do not infer legacy requirements from code.
- Rewrites classify affected semantics/interfaces as delete or preserve.
  **Backward compatibility is opt-in only:** inspect the actual project's root
  engineering docs (root AGENTS.md, ENGINEERING.md or designated root engineering
  policy/README section). The root must affirmatively enable compatibility and
  define its scope; it may delegate details, but a bare link, nested ADR, existing
  client or old test does not enable it. Checked and silent means OFF; inaccessible
  evidence means unknown. No default legacy aliases, shims, additive-only changes,
  versioning, deprecation windows or rolling coexistence. Honor approved removal
  of obsolete code, documents and interfaces; update/remove in-scope imports,
  links and indexes, and actually delete the obsolete files. Do not substitute
  pointer-only files, redirects, shims or grace periods solely to retain old paths.
  Report known affected consumers, including outside the write scope, as concrete
  consequences, not an automatic preservation veto when compatibility is OFF.
  Investigate unknown required current/outside-consumer contracts proportionately;
  mark them `UNVERIFIED` and pause only dependent work, never assume consumers
  disposable. Opt-out is not permission for unrelated deletion, data loss, new
  scope or unsafe effects. Current dependency/API suitability, security, recovery,
  authorized availability and audit/data-retention duties remain distinct duties.
  Stop an affected unit on a genuine current contract defect and revise through
  its authorized owner.
- Scientific ideation bypasses engineering roadmaps, TDD, slicing and terminology
  freezes, never authority, safety, data integrity or resource controls.
